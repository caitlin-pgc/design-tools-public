Colour wheel

Paste hex codes (any separator, with or without #). Each colour is placed by its hue (segment 1–36) and by how light or dark it is: lighter than the pure colour sits in the pale outer band, darker sits nearer the centre. Duller colours are marked “muted”.

Click a colour on the wheel or in the list to see its analogous colours.

    The wheel is based on Don Jusko's Real Color Wheel, from a photo by Michael Hernandez (CC BY 2.0).
